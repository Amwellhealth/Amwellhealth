The American Muslim Well-Being and Experiences in Life Study

  • Examines trauma prevalence and exposure among American Muslim emerging adults.
  • Investigates risk factors and experiences that may influence psychological well-being.
  • Explores the role of culturally and religiously relevant experiences in mental health.
  • Contributes to addressing gaps in psychological research involving American Muslim populations.
