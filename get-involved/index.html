Consultation Services

AMWELL supports community education and knowledge-sharing through consultations, educational resources, and professional outreach. Areas of consultation may include educational and behavioral concerns among K–12 students, culturally responsive mental health education, and access to relevant psychological resources.

Community Partnerships

AMWELL welcomes opportunities to collaborate with community organizations, educational institutions, and other partners through educational talks, workshops, community events, and outreach initiatives. Our goal is to make psychological research and mental health knowledge more accessible while building meaningful relationships with the communities we seek to serve.
